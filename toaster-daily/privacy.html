toaster-daily privacy policy and terms

toaster-daily is a small personal tool. It uploads short videos made by the owner of the YouTube channel the daydreaming toaster (@daydreamtoaster) to that same channel, and once a week tells the owner how many views those videos got.

Terms of use

toaster-daily uses YouTube API Services. By using toaster-daily, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else, and it has no users other than the owner.

What it accesses

It asks Google for two permissions on the owner's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API):

  • upload scope: to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel;
  • read-only scope: to read the public view, like and comment counts of the owner's own recent uploads, 24 and 72 hours after each upload.

It does not read viewers' data, the text of comments, subscribers, or any other channel.

What it stores, and where

  • The OAuth client ID, client secret and refresh token are stored in a local file on the owner's own computer. The refresh token is sent only to Google's OAuth token endpoint, to get a short-lived access token. The access token is sent only to Google, with each YouTube API request.
  • toaster-daily does not keep the video ID or URL. After an upload it reads the response only to check that the upload succeeded and is public. To avoid uploading the same video twice, it keeps its own local note: the date and its own name for the video.
  • To read the counts, it looks at the owner's own recent uploads, picks the one uploaded at the time of its own post, and reads that video's view, like and comment counts. It keeps only those counts, with its own name for the video and the time it read them, in a local file on the owner's computer.
  • Once a week it sends a short summary of these counts (together with counts from the owner's other social media accounts) to the owner's own Telegram chat, through the owner's own Telegram bot. That message is then also subject to Telegram's privacy policy. Nothing from YouTube is sent anywhere else or shared with anyone.
  • The videos, titles and descriptions it uploads are published on YouTube by the owner's choice.

Retention and deletion

  • The tokens are kept until the owner removes them.
  • Counts read from YouTube are deleted from the owner's computer within 30 days after they were read (both the local log and the saved copy of the weekly summary). No other data from YouTube is kept.
  • To stop the tool and delete its access: revoke the permission at Google Account permissions, then delete the YouTube entry and the stored YouTube counts from the local files (the owner does this within 30 days of revoking; in practice right away). Revoking the permission makes the stored refresh token useless.
  • Deleting the local data does not delete videos already on YouTube. Videos are deleted in YouTube Studio.

Ads and analytics

This tool and this page use no ads and no analytics.
